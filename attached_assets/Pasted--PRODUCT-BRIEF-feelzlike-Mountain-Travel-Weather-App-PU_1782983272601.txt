# PRODUCT BRIEF: feelzlike (Mountain Travel Weather App)
# PURPOSE: AI Generation Prompt for Code / Data Research / Copywriting

## 1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW & VALUE PROPOSITION
- **App Name:** feelzlike
- **URL:** https://feelzlike.com
- **Tagline / Philosophy:** Towns first, mountains second.
- **Core Problem Solved:** Weather in alpine resort towns does not match the actual conditions at the mountain peaks. Drivers and transit riders face unpredictable road closures, chain requirements, high winds, and visibility drops.
- **Key Features:** Live weather, real-time road conditions, chain/transit alerts, wind/temperature monitoring, lift status, and live webcam streams.
- **Modes of Transport Covered:** Car and Bus.
- **Target Regions:** Australia (Snowy Mountains, Victoria's High Country), New Zealand (Queenstown, Wanaka), Japan (Nagano/Yamanouchi, Niigata, Hokkaido).

## 2. BRANDING & VISUAL IDENTITY
- **Logo Aesthetics:** Minimalist geometric blue mountain silhouette with a bright blue winding river/road slicing through it forming an 'S' shape. 
- **Typography Style:** Lowercase, clean, sans-serif ("feelzlike").
- **Primary Color Palette:** Deep matte black background, gradient sky blue to royal blue for the alpine peaks, and electric/cyan blue for the road/river wave.

## 3. ADVERTISING STRATEGY & ASSET REQUIREMENTS
We are launching a physical and digital ad campaign to capture travelers before they head up the peaks. The assets needed must focus heavily on the contrast between "Town Conditions" vs. "Mountain Realities".

### A. Physical Stickers (Buses, Transit Hubs, Ski Rental Counters)
- **Format:** Small, high-impact, square or circular vinyl.
- **Required Elements:** Logo, high-contrast QR code placeholder, transport icons (Car, Bus), and ultra-short hook copy.

### B. Posters (A4 & A3 for Information Centers & Lodges)
- **Format:** Static vertical display, eye-level reading.
- **Required Elements:** Logo, dual feature breakdown (1. Live Cams & Wind, 2. Roads & Bus Info), persuasive body copy, QR code.

### C. Outdoor Static Billboards (Highways, Mountain Approach Roads)
- **Format:** Horizontal large-format. Must be readable by moving vehicles in 3 seconds.
- **Required Elements:** Massive font size, high-contrast, Logo on left, URL (feelzlike.com) on bottom right. No QR codes.

### D. Digital Screen Loops (Supermarkets, Malls, Convenience Stores)
- **Format:** 10-second vertical/horizontal video loops.
- **Required Elements:** Split-screen or frame-by-frame contrast ("Sunny in town" vs. "Whiteout on peak"), live UI data visualization, logo animation outro.
