This summary is designed to serve as a high-level developer brief to ensure your app covers all critical ski-specific metrics and leverages the right data sources.1. Core Feature & Data RequirementsDevelopers must prioritize elevation-banded data (Base, Mid, Summit) over standard city forecasts.Precipitation & Snowpack:New Snowfall: 24h, 48h, and 72h predicted accumulation.Base Depth: Total snow depth at the resort.Freezing Level: A dynamic tracker to identify where snow turns to rain.Mountain Conditions:Wind vs. Gusts: Visual alerts for wind thresholds that might trigger lift holds.Visibility & Cloud Cover: Essential for navigating "flat light" conditions.Operational Data:Lift & Trail Status: Real-time percentage of open terrain and specific lift status.Webcams: Integration of resort-provided live image feeds.Safety:Avalanche Danger: Regional danger ratings (1-5 scale) and official forecast links.2. Recommended APIs & ServicesEncourage your team to evaluate these specialized providers, as standard weather APIs often lack mountain-specific granularity.Service CategoryProvider OptionsKey FeaturesSki-Specific WeatherWeather Unlocked, Ski Resort Forecast (RapidAPI)Elevation-specific data, snowfall predictions for 3,000+ resorts.General MeteorologyStormglass.io, OpenWeatherMap, Tomorrow.ioHigh-resolution data blending and easy RESTful integration.Operational & StatsSki API, Ski Resorts Info APIReal-time lift/trail status and resort facility metadata.Avalanche DataSnowSafe, Avalanche.report (Open Data)API access to reports and map layers for safety forecasting.3. Logistical Modules (Optional but Valuable)If adding secondary services, focus on high-utility items that impact the user's ability to reach the mountain.Transport (High Priority): Road conditions and closures are more vital than food or lodging. Use government transit APIs (e.g., State DOTs in the US) or Google Maps APIs for real-time traffic and travel times.Accommodation & Dining (Low Priority): If including these, use Yelp or Tripadvisor for local village info. Keep this in a separate "Village" or "Explore" tab to avoid cluttering the primary weather dashboard.
