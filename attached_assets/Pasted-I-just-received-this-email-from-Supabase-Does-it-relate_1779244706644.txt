What it means
This is not automatically a bug, and it does not necessarily mean the rebuild work here caused it. Supabase’s guidance is that Disk IO alerts usually point to a project-level workload issue such as heavy reads/writes, inefficient queries, or a compute tier that is too small for the traffic or data pattern the database is handling.

It could absolutely be connected to your current snow/weather product if that Supabase project is the backend powering forecast reads, cached weather writes, cron-style updates, or other frequent database activity. The project name “Daily Snow Forecast” makes that connection look very plausible.
