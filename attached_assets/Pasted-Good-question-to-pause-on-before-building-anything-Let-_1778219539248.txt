Good question to pause on before building anything. Let me lay out the landscape so you can pick a direction — no code changes yet, this is a "decide what we want" question.

What we have today
AU: BOM IDR403 radar, animated 6-frame loop, proxied through our API server. National weather service feed — same source every Australian app uses. 128km range from Captain's Flat near Canberra.
JP: RainViewer (free aggregator that re-serves JMA's radar mesh, plus nowcast frames out to 30 min) overlaid on a CartoDB basemap, plus OpenWeatherMap tiles for clouds/temp/snow/rain on the same page.
API key in pocket: OpenWeatherMap (precipitation, snow, temp, clouds, pressure, wind tile layers — all already accessible to us).
What the competitors actually use

The key insight: the gold-standard ski apps don't differentiate on radar — they differentiate on modelled snowfall + snow/rain line. Snow-Forecast built a whole business on a heatmap that says "20cm Wed night above 1500m" — radar is almost a secondary thing.

Our options, ranked by effort
Quick win — Windy.com embed (1-2 hours)
Replace our radar page with https://embed.windy.com/embed2.html?overlay=radar&lat=… configured per-region. Free, no key, professional polish, includes time animation + altitude switcher + multiple overlays. Downside: external iframe, slightly off-brand.

Stay native, upgrade the layers (~½ day)
Keep BOM/RainViewer as the live layer, but add an OpenWeatherMap snowfall-rate tile + temperature-isolines tile on top so you can see precip type and intensity, not just rainfall reflectivity. This is what BOM alone can't show you — radar can't tell snow from rain. Uses our existing OWM key.

The Snow-Forecast play (~1-2 days)
Build a modelled snowfall heatmap from Open-Meteo's hourly snowfall grid (we already use Open-Meteo for hourly forecasts). Render it as a coloured overlay across the region with a 24h/48h/7-day toggle. This is the differentiator that actually helps people plan ski days. Free, no new API, our own brand.

Pro-tier upgrade — paid commercial radar (~$50-500/mo)

Tomorrow.io — best-in-class 0-2hr nowcast, ~$0-100/mo for our volume
AerisWeather (Vaisala Xweather) — what serious weather apps use, ~$200+/mo
Only worth it if (1) and (2) and (3) aren't enough.
OpenSnow partnership — they have an embed widget for resorts. Worth investigating if we want to white-label rather than build.

My recommendation
Do (2) + (3) together. Keep BOM/JMA as the "what's happening right now" layer, add the OWM snowfall + temp overlay so AU users can see snow-vs-rain instantly, and build the Open-Meteo modelled-snowfall heatmap as the headline "Snow Forecast" view. That mirrors what Snow-Forecast and OpenSnow actually do, uses APIs we already pay nothing extra for, stays on-brand, and is the bit that converts a casual skier into a paying subscriber. Windy embed (1) is a sensible 1-day fallback if you want something better this week while we build (2)+(3).
