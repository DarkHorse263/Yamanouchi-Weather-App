# Updated Monetary Thresholds in CCPA

Effective 1/1/2025; posted 12/17/2024

Every odd-numbered year, Civil Code § 1798.199.95(d) adjusts the following monetary thresholds in the
CCPA to reflect increases in the Consumer Price Index. Effective January 1, 2025, the new amounts are as
follows:

| Civil Code § | Updated 2025 Amount | Previous Amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Civil Code § 1798.140(d)(1)(A):**<br>**_Annual gross revenue amount within the definition of “business”_** | **$26,625,000** | **$25,000,000** |
| **Civil Code § 1798.150(a)(1)(A):**<br>**_Monetary damages range per consumer per incident_** | _Not less than **$107** and not greater than **$799** per consumer_<br>_per incident or actual damages,_<br>_whichever is greater._ | _Not less than **$100** and not greater than **$750** per consumer_<br>_per incident or actual damages,_<br>_whichever is greater._ |
| **Civil Code § 1798.155(a):**<br>**_Administrative fine amounts_** | _Not more than **$2,663** for each violation or **$7,988** for each_<br>_intentional violation and violations_<br>_involving the personal information of consumers whom the violator has actual knowledge are under_<br>_16_<br>_years of age._ | _Not more than **$2,500** for each violation or **$7,500** for each_<br>_intentional violation and violations_<br>_involving the personal information of consumers whom the violator has actual knowledge are under_<br>_16_<br>_years of age._ |
| **Civil Code § 1978.199.90(a):**<br>**_Civil penalty amounts_** | _Not more than **$2,663** for each violation or **$7,988** for each_<br>_intentional violation and violations_<br>_involving the personal information of consumers whom the violator has actual knowledge are under_<br>_16_<br>_years of age._ | _Not more than **$2,500** for each violation or **$7,500** for each_<br>_intentional violation and violations_<br>_involving the personal information of consumers whom the violator has actual knowledge are under_<br>_16_<br>_years of age._ |
| **Civil Code § 1798.199.25:**<br>**_Daily compensation rate for Board members_** | _$107_ | _$100_ |
| 1 In accordance with the law, the Agency uses the Consumer Price Index<br> (CPI)—California, All Items, All Urban Consumers percentage change from the previous two years'<br> reports, published by the Department of Industrial Relations, Office of the Director–Research.<br> The agency applies the percentage change in the CPI for the August-to-August point in time of the<br> prior two years. The increase in the thresholds is rounded to the nearest whole dollar. |
