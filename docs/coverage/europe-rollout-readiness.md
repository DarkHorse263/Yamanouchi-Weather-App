# Europe-wide rollout: inventory and release gates

Updated 7 October 2026. Owner target: 31 October 2026.

## Scope

The owner's instruction is "Any with ski resorts and mountains".
Do not reduce this to a handful of well-known ski resorts, the EU, or only
the Alps. France and Italy are the first batches, not the final inclusion set.

The country ledger is a discovery checklist, not a verified resort catalogue.
It includes small countries and geographic boundary cases so exclusions are
deliberate rather than accidental. Boundary cases are not automatically
approved for European-labelled public pages. Dependent territories and
subnational areas found during discovery must be attached to the appropriate
country with explicit geographic labels; do not silently discard them.

Research mountain destinations as well as downhill resorts. A mountain
destination is not automatically a ski area, and an arbitrary named peak is
not automatically a useful destination page. Record Nordic-only, indoor,
synthetic, closed and proposed facilities separately. Never show outdoor snow
forecasts as indoor conditions or invent ski lifts for mountain-only pages.

## Counting and evidence

For every candidate, establish:

1. Canonical name, aliases, country, region and facility/destination type.
2. Official operator, tourism or public-authority evidence, with check date.
3. Current operating identity and any closure or relocation uncertainty.
4. Connected-network relationships without duplicate resort counts.
5. Village coordinates and sourced elevation, independent of terrain heights.
6. Suitable forecast coordinates, timezone and explicitly chosen forecast level.
7. Terrain/base/upper elevations where evidenced; no guessed values.
8. Official arrival, resort report and safety-information links.
9. Data-source permission and attribution status for any integrated content.
10. An explicit verified, unresolved or excluded disposition with reasons.

Published page counts, candidate counts and verified resort counts must remain
separate. The initial country checklist must not be used as a completion
percentage or as proof that every listed country has eligible resorts.

## Implementation batches

1. Tignes and Val d'Isère; Val Gardena / Seiser Alm and Alta Badia.
2. Broader France/Italy, Austria and Switzerland inventories and verified batches.
3. Germany, Liechtenstein, Iberia, Nordic countries and Britain/Ireland.
4. Central/eastern Europe, the Balkans and Baltic countries.
5. Remaining small-country and geographic-boundary reviews.

This is sequencing, not a dated capacity forecast. Do not wait until an entire
large country is complete to process evidence-ready destinations elsewhere.
Keep all unresolved areas visible in the inventory.

## Required runtime checks for an included destination

- Working country discovery, search, map pin and canonical route.
- Distinct town and on-mountain forecasts where both are supported.
- Correct timezone, units, forecast elevation and snowfall-phase handling.
- Honest unavailable, stale and provider-failure states.
- Alert eligibility only for genuine supported outdoor snow forecasts.
- Official arrival guidance; no invented timetable or live road status.
- Official report/camera links unless an integration is permissioned and tested.
- Mobile rendering, direct navigation, SEO and backend/frontend registry parity.
- Attribution and provider plan capacity checked against expanded usage.

Mountain-only and non-outdoor facilities need appropriate feature exclusions,
not forced ski-resort parity. Forecasts never establish safe access or skiability.

## Completion and deadline

31 October is the requested target, not a verified promise that every European
destination can be researched, implemented, tested and published by that date.
No per-day delivery quantities have been established.

Full coverage cannot be declared while national inventory reconciliation or
material destination gaps remain unresolved. If the target date arrives first,
describe the release as partial and report the outstanding countries and
destinations. Do not quietly redefine "complete" to mean "some pages per country".

Publication remains a separate owner action. Existing public deployment state
has not been audited as part of this scope update.

## Source checks completed in this scope pass

### Tignes / Val d'Isère

Source: https://en.tignes.net/skiing/ski-area

The official page describes the connected Tignes–Val d'Isère area and links its
official piste map at https://en.tignes.net/skiing/ski-area/ski-map.
Its prose gives an upper altitude of 3,450 m while its statistics show 3,456 m.
The 300 km and 74 lifts on that page are not independent totals for each village.
Resolve upper terrain height and local forecast points before importing values.
Neither the page nor seasonal marketing copy proves current winter operation.

### Italian pilot identities

Source: https://www.dolomitisuperski.com/en/areas

The official directory lists 12 named areas, including the separate pilot entries:

- https://www.dolomitisuperski.com/en/areas/alta-badia
- https://www.dolomitisuperski.com/en/areas/val-gardena-alpe-di-siusi

The fetched page mixes winter destination descriptions with a Supersummer
heading and current lift totals. Do not import those totals as local winter
skiing status, or turn the shared pass into one forecast location. Local terrain,
village, winter arrival and permission evidence remains to be completed.
