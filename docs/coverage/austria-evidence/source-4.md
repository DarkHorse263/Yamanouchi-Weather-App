### Top hits for families

- In the Großglockner/Heiligenblut ski region children under 10 ski with a “Snowman Ticket” for just 10 Euro per day.
- "Family Euro" at Bad Kleinkirchheim/St. Oswald region: Children up to 12 years of age can ski for just 1 Euro per day.
- Nassfeld: stay 4 nights / pay for 3 nights, ski pass 3 days for the price of 2 days
- “Ski for free” at Weissensee
- Children up to 14 years of age ski free at Sportberg Goldeck.
