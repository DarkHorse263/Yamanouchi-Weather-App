### Hochkar ski area

The Hochkar presents itself as the **highest ski area in Lower Austria** with a distinctly high mountain character and long seasons.

- **Character**: Lower Austria's most snow-sure ski area, 19 km of slopes
- **Highlights**: Panoramic summit views of the Ybbstal Alps, sun terraces, rustic huts with regional cuisine
- **Adrenalin factor**: Powder paradise for freeriders, sporty red and black slopes

### Semmering Hirschenkogel ski area

The Hirschenkogel on the Semmering is an urban classic with **World Cup history** and a strong **evening programme**. The combination of railway backdrop, night skiing and toboggan run creates a special winter atmosphere.

- **Character**: Around 14 km of pistes, Europe's most extensive night-time piste offering with 6 slopes
- **Refreshment tip**: [Liechtensteinhaus panorama restaurant](https://www.lower-austria.info/gastronomy/panoramarestaurant-liechtensteinhaus)
- **Extras**: Good public transport connections by train and bus, 3 km toboggan run with floodlights

### Lackenhof am Ötscher ski area

Lackenhof am Ötscher is a **quiet mountain village** at the foot of the " **Vaterberg of the Mostviertel**" and offers a relaxed setting for skiing fun. Beginners, advanced skiers and leisurely carvers will find gentle access to alpine terrain here.

- **Character**: family atmosphere, wide blue runs, sporty slopes on the Großer Ötscher
- **Perspectives:** View of the Großer and Kleiner Ötscher, wintry forest slopes, idyllic mountain village without the hustle and bustle
- **Off-slope**: 12 km of hiking trails, 2 signposted snowshoe tours, 30 km of groomed cross-country ski trails, ski touring offers

- #### Which ski resorts are easily accessible by public transport?






Most ski areas are easily accessible by public transport.



**In the Mostviertel:** To the [**Gemeindealpe**](https://www.lower-austria.info/excursions/gemeindealpe-mitterbach-ski-resort) can be easily reached with the Mariazell railway from St. Pölten (from the Mitterbach Gemeindealpe stop it is approx. 7-10 minutes on foot), as well as to [**Annaberg**](https://www.lower-austria.info/excursions/skigebiet-annaberger-lifte) in combination with a regional bus. The " [Bus to the Snow](https://blaguss.com/reiseangebot/mit-dem-bus-zum-schnee)" service takes guests comfortably from Vienna via St. Pölten and Ybbs to the [**Hochkar**](https://www.lower-austria.info/excursions/hochkar-ski-area).



**In the Vienna Alps:** the [**Semmering/Hirschenkogel**](https://www.lower-austria.info/excursions/semmering-hirschenkogel) can be reached directly by train via the Semmering railway, from the station you can walk or take the bus to the mountain railway. Other ski resorts in Lower Austria are also increasingly relying on public transport. To the [**Unterberg**](https://www.lower-austria.info/excursions/schizentrum-unterberg) can be reached by train via Wiener Neustadt main station to Pernitz-Muggendorf station. From there, a [shuttle bus](https://www.schigebiet-unterberg.at/kontakt/) will take you to the ski area. The [**Erlebnisalm Mönichkirchen**](https://www.lower-austria.info/excursions/schigebiet-moenichkirchen-mariensee) is also easily accessible by public transport.

- #### Where can I do winter sports after dark?






At [Semmering](https://www.lower-austria.info/excursions/semmering-hirschenkogel), a large part of the pistes are floodlit for **night skiing**; [Annaberg](https://www.lower-austria.info/excursions/skigebiet-annaberger-lifte) offers **ski touring evenings** and **night tobogganing**; in other regions there are occasional night skiing and ski touring evenings. We recommend taking a look at the events calendar of the Lower Austrian ski resorts.

- #### Are there ski resorts with a strong focus on nature?






The [Unterberg](https://www.lower-austria.info/excursions/schizentrum-unterberg) **natural snow paradise** does without extensive snowmaking and favours natural snow, manageable infrastructure and plenty of peace and quiet. In several areas, [hiking trails](https://www.lower-austria.info/hiking-in-winter), cross-country ski trails and [snowshoe routes](https://www.lower-austria.info/snowshoeing) invite you to experience winter away from the piste.

- #### What price advantages do season and network tickets offer in the Lower Austrian ski resorts?






Several card models bundle winter sports resorts and thus create attractive price advantages. The [mountain adventure pass](https://www.bergerlebnispass.at/) gives you access to ten ski areas with 125 kilometres of pistes for one winter season - ideal for anyone who is regularly out and about in the mountains and wants to explore different areas.



In addition, the ["Skigebiete im Mariazellerland](https://www.mostviertel.at/mariazellerland-skigebiete) " network connects the Annaberg, Mariazeller Bürgeralpe and Gemeindealpe Mitterbach ski areas so that one lift pass is valid in all three areas.



The [Ostalpen-Card](https://www.skiregion-ostalpen.at/) is a cross-province advantage card that combines several ski regions in Lower Austria and Styria in one season ticket.

- #### What does "dynamic pricing" mean for ski tickets?






Dynamic pricing refers to dynamic pricing for ski passes: **ticket prices are not fixed**, but _change depending on the time of booking_, demand, capacity utilisation or seasonal period.



Those who book early or are travelling on less popular days often pay less, while prices can be higher on busy days. The aim is to improve capacity utilisation at the ski resorts - for guests, this ideally means **early booking benefits** and **cheaper tickets outside the high season**.

- #### Where can ski passes be booked or reserved online?






Day and multi-day tickets can be booked directly via the mountain railway online ticket shops in many Lower Austrian ski resorts. Depending on the area, print-at-home tickets, QR codes for smartphones or advance bookings with collection at the ticket office are also possible.



It is best to take a look at the respective websites of [Hochkar](https://shop.hochkar.com/hochkar), [Ötscher/Lackenhof](https://shop.oetscher.at/oetscher), [Annaberg](https://shop.annaberg.info/annaberg) and [Erlebnisalm Mönichkirchen](https://shop.erlebnisalm.com/moenichkirchen) \- there you will find current online offers as well as a transparent price overview.
