## The Warth-Schröcken ski resort

With plenty of natural snow, Warth-Schröcken ranks among the snowiest ski resorts in Europe. This Arlberg ski region blends authenticity with international skiing standards and has something for everyone – from [freeride](https://www.skiarlberg.at/en/warth-schroecken/winter/freeriden) adventures and [family-friendly offers](https://www.skiarlberg.at/en/warth-schroecken/winter/family-winter) to Alpine culinary delights at traditional [mountain huts](https://www.skiarlberg.at/en/warth-schroecken/culinary-experiences).

## Freeride paradise Warth-Schröcken

With over 200 kilometres of off-piste variants and snow reliability stretching deep into spring, Warth-Schröcken stands as one of the Alps’ premier freeride destinations. Untouched slopes, guided tours and professional safety training turn the region into a true haven for powder enthusiasts.

The longest ski circuit in the Alps

## RUN of FAME

Eighty-five kilometres, 18,000 vertical metres, and three impressive mountain passes – the " [Run of Fame](https://www.skiarlberg.at/en/winter/run-of-fame)" is the most spectacular ski circuit in the Alps. Crossing the Arlberg, Flexen, and Hochtannberg passes, the route links St. Anton/Rendl, Stuben, Zürs, Lech, Schröcken, and Warth, creating a truly unique skiing experience. As a tribute to ski pioneers and stars from sport and film, the RUN of FAME captivates with its many different slopes, breathtaking panoramas, and Alpine history.

[More about the RUN of FAME](https://www.skiarlberg.at/en/winter/run-of-fame)

## For adrenaline junkies

Warth-Schröcken has more to offer than just perfect slopes: On the Steffisalp [fun slope](https://www.skiarlberg.at/en/warth-schroecken/winter/highlights-in-the-ski-resort/fun-park-fun-slope), the [funcross Karhorn](https://www.skiarlberg.at/en/warth-schroecken/winter/highlights-in-the-ski-resort/funcross-karhorn), or the Salober [parallel giant slalom](https://www.skiarlberg.at/en/warth-schroecken/winter/highlights-in-the-ski-resort/parallel-giant-slalom), skiing skills are put to the test. The [skimovie](https://www.skiarlberg.at/en/warth-schroecken/winter/highlights-in-the-ski-resort/skimovie) course records every run, while the [speed check](https://www.skiarlberg.at/en/warth-schroecken/winter/highlights-in-the-ski-resort/racing-course-speed-check) at the Kuchlbahn is all about hundredths of a second. Thrill-seekers are sure to discover their ultimate winter highlight here. The "Ochsakopf" black ski run on the Salober, Warth-Schröcken’s steepest slope, offers a particularly challenging descent. It is a must for experienced skiers.

## Fun in the snow beyond the slopes

In Warth-Schröcken, a wide range of winter activities await you beyond the ski slopes. Three [toboggan runs](https://www.skiarlberg.at/en/warth-schroecken/winter/tobogganing) guarantee speedy fun. Meanwhile, those who prefer a quieter pace can discover the snow-covered landscape on idyllic [winter trails](https://www.skiarlberg.at/en/warth-schroecken/winter/winter-hiking). The diversity of the mountain landscape can also be explored beyond the slopes.

## Great food & drink in Warth-Schröcken

From regional specialities to inventive cuisine, the area’s [restaurants & bars](https://www.skiarlberg.at/en/warth-schroecken/culinary-experiences) blends Alpine tradition with modern dining. Numerous restaurants and ski huts on the slopes invite you to take a culinary break, offering everything from hearty classics to creative dishes. There’s something to delight every taste and create a personal moment of enjoyment.
