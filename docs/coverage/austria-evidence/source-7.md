## Ski Arlberg: Austria’s largest ski resort

Ski Arlberg stands for boundless variety, outstanding snow conditions and skiing on a grand scale – from wide family-friendly runs to legendary freeride terrain. A seamless network of lifts and cable cars connects St. Anton – St. Christoph – Stuben, Lech – Oberlech – Zürs and Warth-Schröcken.

With 301 kilometres of slopes and 85 lifts and cable cars, Ski Arlberg is Austria’s largest ski resort and one of the country’s leading winter sports destinations. Internationally, it ranks among the five largest ski resorts in the world and attracts winter sports enthusiasts from across the globe.

## A variety of slopes for all skill levels

In Ski Arlberg, the terrain caters to every ambition: from first turns on easy beginner slopes to adrenaline-fuelled descents through deep powder snow. What sets the ski resort apart is its sheer size and cutting-edge infrastructure, seamlessly connecting [St. Anton – St. Christoph – Stuben](https://www.skiarlberg.at/en/st-anton), [Lech – Oberlech – Zürs](https://www.skiarlberg.at/en/lech-zuers) and [Warth-Schröcken](https://www.skiarlberg.at/en/warth-schroecken). This exceptional variety is one of the reasons Ski Arlberg ranks among Austria’s leading ski resorts.

**Ski runs & slopes in Ski Arlberg**

- Wide blue runs for beginners
- Long red runs, ideal for carving
- Challenging black runs
- Legendary freeride routes

In addition to the 300 kilometres of slopes, several snow parks, fun parks, race courses, speed checks and the [RUN of FAME](https://www.skiarlberg.at/en/winter/run-of-fame) add even more variety.

## Freeriding at its finest

Ski Arlberg receives an average of 9 metres of natural snowfall per season, creating ideal conditions for unforgettable powder days. More than 200 kilometres of off-piste routes give experienced freeriders plenty of space to seek out fresh lines and enjoy the freedom of skiing away from the groomed ski runs.

Local ski guides know the backcountry inside out and are familiar with the on-site snow conditions. With their experience and local knowledge, they can show you some of the best lines and hidden spots across the Arlberg region.

A ski resort steeped in history

## Legend of the Alps

Ski Arlberg is as vast as it is legendary. In the place where alpine skiing has its roots, tradition and trailblazing spirit have gone hand in hand from the very beginning. Fuelled by a passion for winter sports, the Arlberg gave rise to ideas that have made history and innovations that have defined its legacy.

The founding of the Alp’s first ski club, the first ski lesson and the first ski race all originated on the Arlberg. This authentic heritage can still be felt today: in the character of the villages, on historic runs and in the infectious enthusiasm of the people who call the Arlberg home.

## Skiing fun for the whole family

A delight for young beginners: several children’s areas and practice lifts make Ski Arlberg an ideal place to learn to ski. Wide runs, family-friendly infrastructure and special attractions such as the Hopplweg slope on Gampen or the children’s fun park in Warth-Schröcken create perfect conditions for relaxed family skiing.

And there is plenty to experience away from the ski runs too: Toboggan runs and winter hiking trails offer even more ways to enjoy time together amid the snow-covered Alpine scenery.

## Awards and accolades for Ski Arlberg

Ski Arlberg is widely recognised as one of Europe’s greatest ski resorts and ranks among the world’s best-known winter sports destinations. Its exceptional quality, variety and appeal are affirmed by those who regularly carve its slopes as well as numerous independent awards and accolades. ## Frequently asked questions

- Ski area


Where is the Arlberg?


The Arlberg region lies on the border between the Austrian provinces of Vorarlberg and Tyrol. It is easy to reach by car, train or bus, with several airports located around 100 to 300 kilometres away.

How many kilometres of slopes does Austria’s largest ski resort have?


Ski Arlberg connects the three regions of St. Anton – St. Christoph – Stuben, Lech – Oberlech – Zürs and Warth-Schröcken. It is Austria’s largest ski resort and ranks among the eight largest worldwide.

The ski resort offers:

- 301 km of slopes for all skill levels
- more than 200 km of powder-snow descents and extensive freeride terrain
- 85 state-of-the-art cable cars and lifts

How high is the Arlberg?


The Arlberg is a mountain pass located at 1,793 metres above sea level on the border between Vorarlberg and Tyrol. The highest point in the Ski Arlberg ski resort is Valluga’s peak at 2,811 metres.

The ski resort extends from 1,304 to 2,811 metres above sea level.

What is Austria’s largest ski resort?


Austria’s largest ski resort is Ski Arlberg, with 301 kilometres of slopes and 85 cable cars and lifts.
