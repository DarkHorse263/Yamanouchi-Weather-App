# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Home page and public landing pages
- Country landing pages (`/au`, `/jp`, `/nz`, `/countries`)
- Public weather discovery pages (`/near-you`, `/news`, `/plan`)
- Public region, town, and mountain pages under `/:region/**`
- Public legal pages (`/legal/**`)

## Out of scope
- Authenticated or admin pages (`/admin/**`)
- Transactional token-management routes (`/alerts/verify`, `/alerts/manage`, `/alerts/unsubscribed`, `/newsletter/verify`, `/newsletter/unsubscribed`)
- API endpoints except where they affect crawlability or public asset serving

## Target audience
- Travelers, skiers, snowboarders, and outdoor visitors researching resort towns and mountain conditions in Australia and Japan.

## Primary keywords
- Weather for resort towns
- Snowy Mountains weather
- Yamanouchi weather
- Ski town weather
- Mountain weather and conditions
