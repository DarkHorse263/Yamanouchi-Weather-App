---
name: feelzlike webcam sourcing
description: The approved sourcing and honesty rules for live-camera coverage.
---

Prefer official resort, public road/weather, municipal, and tourism-authority cameras. Stable public YouTube streams and third-party networks may be used when their operator and viewpoint are explicit; aggregators are link-out discovery fallbacks rather than copied media.

**Why:** A camera can be useful without showing the ski hill. Treating a village, station, or access-road view as mountain evidence would misrepresent current slope conditions and ownership.

**How to apply:** Record the exact location, vantage category, source/operator, official or third-party status, live/refresh mode, last verification date, and an external fallback. Embed only when permitted and stable. Credit the underlying operator rather than the directory where the camera was discovered.
