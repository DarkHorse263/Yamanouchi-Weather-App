---
name: Regional subscription pricing
description: Regional pricing intent and why address changes must not revoke paid access.
---

Use deliberate regional price points rather than live exchange-rate conversion. Keep the billing-country choice independent of the ski region being viewed.

**Why:** The owner wants US and eurozone subscriptions priced for those markets, not cheaper foreign-currency equivalents of AUD. Travelling to a ski destination does not change a customer's billing market.

**How to apply:** Preserve the subscription's purchased currency through renewals and country changes. Validate approved server-owned prices and ownership, but never charge and then withhold access solely because the Stripe billing address differs from the declared pricing country. Stripe's actual billing address governs tax; moving address is not grounds for revoking paid entitlement.
